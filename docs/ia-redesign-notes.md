# IA redesign — research notes (2026-05-26)

Internal-only working notes that informed the v1.7.0 reader-journey IA
redesign. Public-facing summary lives in `methodology/00-product-thesis.md`
(section: "Reading order"). This file captures the raw research, the
decisions, and the things we deliberately did not do — so a future
revisit doesn't repeat the literature scan.

## What prompted this

After shipping v1.5.3.1 (calibrated uncertainty) + v1.6.3 (retrospective
framing), the `/diligence/[asset]` page surfaces all the right content
a biotech-VC-grade report needs, but in **module-registry-discovery
order** (PoS → ML PoS → rNPV → Scorecard → Comparables → Calibration)
rather than **reader-journey order**. A senior VC opening the live URL
has to scroll past four panels of math before they see what the
framework's recommendation is.

The diagnostic phrase: the page is structurally a **dashboard** but its
audience expects a **document**. Dashboards are collections of
independent widgets the reader navigates freely; documents are linear
narratives with a deliberate reading order where each section answers
a question generated by the previous one. Bloomberg terminals are
dashboards because traders sample; investment memos are documents
because LPs/IC members consume top-to-bottom and won't reach page 3
unless page 1 earned it.

## Research findings

Three parallel web searches (biotech VC investment-memo structure,
equity research report structure, healthcare VC partner reading
behavior). Sources cited at the bottom.

### Investment memo structure — industry consensus

Across the Bessemer Memos public collection, Visible.vc templates, the
Halioua biotech-specific guide, and the VC Factory IC-memo guide, the
canonical order is:

1. TL;DR / recommendation
2. Investment thesis ("why this works")
3. Team
4. Business / product overview
5. Market analysis
6. Financial / valuation
7. **Risks & mitigants** — described as "the real art of the IC memo"
8. Capital structure / use of funds
9. Appendix

For **biotech specifically**, sections are amended to add: glossary of
terms, longer industry-specific science/regulatory section, deal
structure, and return scenarios.

### Equity research report structure — the closer analog for Asclepius

Asclepius's output shape (single-asset, framework-driven, with
recommendation + price target) maps more cleanly to the equity-research
report than to the early-stage VC memo. M&I's equity-research guide and
the Danske Bank Pharma Equity Group sample establish the pattern:

1. **First page = recommendation banner**: Buy/Hold/Sell + target
   price (range, not point) + recent catalysts. The reader's first
   question is "do I keep reading?", not "show me the math."
2. Executive summary
3. Company overview
4. Industry analysis
5. Financial analysis
6. **Valuation** (method-explained, output as range not point)
7. **Risks** (biotech-specific: FDA, reimbursement, clinical readout
   calendar)
8. Appendix

### Healthcare VC scan-first signals

Per the Spectup and Qubit Capital research, healthcare VC partners
specifically scan for four signals:

- Clarity of problem / mechanism
- Strength of team (+ capital position)
- Evidence of traction (Phase data, designations)
- **Risk awareness** — described as the partner-killer dimension

The illustrative anecdote (Spectup): a $12M healthtech Series A that
got killed in IC because "the deck mentioned nothing about FDA
timelines, reimbursement risk, or healthcare regulatory unknowns." Two
partners with healthcare regulatory experience opened fire with
questions the memo couldn't answer.

Conclusion: risk-section depth is the single most decision-load-bearing
surface for a healthcare-VC reader. A diligence tool that buries
limitations in a sidebar is signaling the wrong priorities.

## Decisions

### Adopted (will ship in v1.7.0)

1. **HeroBanner above the fold** with recommendation chip + rNPV
   range + LOA + reflexivity tier + next-catalyst row. Mirrors
   equity-research first-page convention.
2. **Reader-journey section grouping** (5 beats): Thesis → Valuation →
   Operational → Risk → Action. Each section gets a one-line "Q:"
   microcopy stating what question it answers.
3. **Reflexivity slider promoted** from aside to top-of-Thesis-section,
   full-width, magenta-accented (the differentiator color).
4. **Comparables before Scorecard** in valuation section — number-
   readers anchor rNPV to deal comps before qualitative review.
5. **Risk section consolidated** — LimitationsPanel + AdversaryPanel
   merged into one prominent section. Adversary renders inline when
   output is available, not behind a button.
6. **Asset form collapsible** — moved from 320px aside to a top
   `<details>` strip; default-expanded on first visit, collapsed
   thereafter with a one-line summary. Frees the 320px for full-width
   content.

### Considered + rejected

- **Multiple personas in v1.7.0.** Considered shipping persona toggle
  with the IA redesign. Rejected: scope balloon, each persona is
  non-zero design work. Pushed to v1.8.0.
- **Move to a multi-page app** (e.g., `/diligence/<asset>/summary`,
  `/diligence/<asset>/valuation`, `/diligence/<asset>/risk`).
  Rejected: kills the "scroll through one document" rhythm; loses
  ability to drag-reflexivity-and-watch-rNPV-update live.
- **Adding a TOC / sticky nav.** Rejected for v1.7.0: the 5-section
  page is short enough that a TOC adds visual noise without value.
  Reconsider if a sixth section emerges.
- **Replacing recharts with a different lib.** Rejected: out of scope.
  The IA redesign is layout, not chart-engine.
- **Backend changes.** Hard non-goal. The redesign is a layout-only
  React refactor.

### Deferred (post-v1.7.0)

- **Persona views** → v1.8.0
- **Loom demo recap** — record after both v1.7.0 + v1.8.0 are live
- **PDF export** — listed as a disabled stub in the new action strip;
  proper implementation can either route through the Memo Writer
  agent (its output already is a markdown memo) or use a client-side
  PDF library
- **Methodology parity lint** — separate concern; the IA redesign
  doesn't touch numeric claims
